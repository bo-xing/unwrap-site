# Unwrap website design

## Direction

Content-first product website with a large typographic opening, an illustrative archive, three editorial feature columns, an explicit Free/Pro comparison, and direct support/privacy access. Adopt the UI/UX skill's Minimalism & Swiss Style structure; adapt its generic palette to the actual Unwrap icon.

## Tokens

- Paper: `#f7f8f4`
- Ink: `#192526`
- Secondary text: `#54625f`
- Accent: `#096750`
- Mint surface: `#d3f3e5`
- Pro surface: `#182b2a`
- Pro text: `#eef8f0`
- Borders: `#d7dfd8`
- Font: local system sans-serif with Chinese system fallbacks. No external font requests.
- Main content maximum: 1184px. Reading text maximum: 720px.

## Behavior

- Real links, native disclosure elements, one h1 per page, skip link, visible focus styles.
- Compact header retains support and language links on small screens.
- Language switching preserves page kind.
- No automatic animation. Smooth anchor scrolling is disabled for reduced motion.
- The archive illustration is an accessible image with a descriptive label; its contents are sample data, not real customer files or a claimed App screenshot.

## Verified

2026-09-05: all six pages checked in the browser at 375px and 1440px, without horizontal overflow or missing images. Checked language navigation and opening a support FAQ. Static inspection verified 115 local navigation, anchor and asset links across seven HTML documents, including 404.
